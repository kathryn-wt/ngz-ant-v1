This site is your starting point for understanding WelTel’s comprehensive design guidelines. Whether you’re a designer, developer, or stakeholder, you’ll find the essential principles, components, and best practices needed to build consistent, user-centered experiences.

The WelTel Design System is our single source of truth for interface design and development. It defines shared standards and a common visual language, enabling teams to work more efficiently, reduce errors, and collaborate seamlessly—ultimately enhancing the overall user experience.


## What is a design system?

A design system for a Software as a Service (SaaS) product is a structured collection of reusable components, design standards, and brand guidelines that promote consistency and efficiency across the platform. It serves as a shared language for designers and developers, making it easier to build, scale, and maintain user experiences. Without a design system, teams risk accumulating design debt—redundant, inconsistent patterns that slow development and reduce usability.

WelTel’s design system blends core SaaS design principles with brand identity foundations. It includes a growing library of UI components, accessibility considerations, and usage guidelines that streamline collaboration and reduce repetitive design decisions. This foundation supports a cohesive, scalable, and user-centered experience across all touchpoints.


## How is it structured?

### Who we are

Core values, vision, and mission that guide our design philosophy.

We ensure no one is left behind by designing solutions that serve all individuals, regardless of their circumstances.


### Brand

Visual identity elements such as logo, color palette, iconography, button styles, typography, and tone for marketing and web.


### Platform Design

UI-specific applications of the brand, including layout structure, typography, and voice tailored for the product.


### Components

A library of reusable components—such as buttons, forms, tables, and modals—each with clear documentation on usage guidelines, design principles, code snippets, and configurable properties. The library also includes shared global elements like color tokens, typography styles, and spacing rules to ensure consistency across the platform.
